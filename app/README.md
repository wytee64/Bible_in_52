# BibleIn52

Is a Bible reading tracker app that guides you through a one-year Bible reading plan. It helps you track daily readings, manage progress, and set reminders for consistent reading.

## Features

- **Daily Reading Plan**: Follow a structured weekly plan covering the entire Bible in 52 weeks.
- **Progress Tracking**: Mark each day's reading as completed and see your progress visually.
- **Streak Counter**: Track your consecutive days of completed readings.
- **Reading Statistics**: View the number of books completed, total chapters read, and books in progress.
- **Calendar View**: Check your completed readings on a monthly calendar.
- **Daily Reminders**: Enable notifications at a chosen time to remind you to read.
- **Interactive UI**: Toggle readings on the reading plan, and visually see completed days with color indicators.
- **Predefined Weekly Readings**: Each week contains a curated set of Bible readings covering both Old and New Testament passages.
